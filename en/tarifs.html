01 Does Vigie replace someone on my team?General+

No. It reads and sorts for you; your team keeps the writing, and you keep the approval. It saves the first hour of the day, not a job.

02 Does Vigie publish on its own?General+

Never. Nothing goes out without the director's approval. Once approved, the post opens in the chosen network with the text ready.

Half a day: we set up your firm, declare your fields and sources, and invite the team. The next morning, the first digest is there.
