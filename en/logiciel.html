(02) The software

The software, screen by screen.

What your team opens every morning, on the office computer and on the phone. The images use sample data and show the interface in French.

Six screens

All in one place.

Pick a screen to see it full size, or scroll down to see them one by one.

01

The morning digest

At the time you choose, Vigie has read your sources and sorted them. The home screen shows what's waiting.

  • To approve, at a glance
  • Open tenders
  • What the team did yesterday
02

One card per signal

The title, the source, a score out of 100 and a summary. Your team writes the post there and discusses it.

  • Draft suggested by Vigie
  • One version per network
  • Team conversation
03

Publications

Every card, from draft to published. You see what is waiting for your approval.

  • Filter by status
  • One-click approval
  • Share to your networks
04

Tenders

Public procurement and donor notices in your field, with their deadline.

  • Public procurement portal
  • World Bank, European Union
  • Track every bid
05

The planner

The week in seven columns, one card per post, with its time and network.

  • Add in one click
  • Filter by network
  • Print to PDF
06

Messages

One conversation per teammate. Mentions made on a card show up there too.

  • On your phone as at the office
  • Mentions link to the card
  • Everyone reads only their own messages
