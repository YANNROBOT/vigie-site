01 Does Vigie replace someone on my team?General+

No. It reads and sorts for you; your team keeps the writing, and you keep the approval. It saves the first hour of the day, not a job.

02 Does Vigie publish on its own?General+

Never. Nothing goes out without the director's approval. Once approved, the post opens in the chosen network with the text ready.

03 How long does it take to get started?Getting started+

Half a day: we set up your firm, declare your fields and sources, and invite the team. The next morning, the first digest is there.

04 Does the price include AI sorting?Pricing+

Yes. Sorting and drafts are included in the plan; there is nothing else to pay.

05 Where do the sources come from?Sources+

Official gazettes, public procurement portals, donors (World Bank, European Union), authorities (ARCOP, BCEAO) and the business press. You add your own and star the most important ones.

06 Who sees what in the firm?Team+

Everyone has their own account and role: director, community manager, designer. The director approves; the others write and comment. Messages between two people are read only by them.

07 What about tenders?Sources+

Vigie reads procurement notices and donor calls, keeps those in your field and shows the deadline. You track every bid: to review, in progress, submitted, won.

08 Does it work on a phone?Getting started+

Yes. Vigie installs on a phone's home screen like an app, and alerts you when an important signal comes in.

09 Does my data stay mine?Security+

Yes. Each firm has its own database, separate from the others. You can export everything, and delete everything, from the settings.
