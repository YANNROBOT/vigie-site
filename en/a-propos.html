Vigie. Monitoring that sorts for you.

Monitoring software for firms: every morning it reads the texts that matter, keeps those that concern your field and drafts your posts.

The first hour of the day, given back.

Every morning, someone opened ten sites one by one. Vigie does that reading for them.

Official gazettes, tender portals, donors, press: Vigie reads them all, keeps only what concerns your field, and turns it into one card per signal, with a draft post. Your team takes over where the work matters: writing, choosing, publishing.

Four rules, kept.

What Vigie does, and what it will never do for you.

Nothing goes out without you

Vigie prepares, your team writes, and every post waits for the director's approval.

Your field first

Sorting follows the fields you declare. Each signal is scored out of 100; you only read what concerns you.

The whole team, each with a role

Director, community manager, designer: everyone has an account, and messages between two people stay between them.

Your data stays yours

Each firm has its own database, separate from the others. You can export everything, and delete everything.

The sources that set the rules.

Vigie starts from the sources of your country and your field, and you add your own.

Official gazettesThe laws, decrees and rulings that change the rules.
Public tendersNotices from procurement portals, with their deadline.
DonorsWorld Bank, European Union.
AuthoritiesRegulators and central banks, such as ARCOP or the BCEAO.
Business pressNewspapers and news sites.
Your sourcesThe ones you add, the most important starred.

Half a day to get started.

You declare your fields and sources, and invite the team.

The next morning, the first digest is there, on the office computer and on the phone. Vigie installs on Windows, and on a phone's home screen like an app.
