(09) About

Deilex. Structuring impact.

Deilex Audit & Conseils is an institutional consulting firm based in Abidjan. It designed Vigie, first for its own monitoring.

Who we are

Where the law meets the ground.

Deilex works at the crossroads of law, strategy, financing and territorial development.

We are not a sector firm: we are the one that understands what a sector does to the law, to public powers and to contracts. Our work starts with a careful reading of the region's texts, and ends with a decision our clients can act on.

Deilex Audit & Conseils — Structuring impact, transforming Africa.

Our four pillars

Four areas, one method.

The same four pillars shape our advice and Vigie's editorial line.

P1

Regulatory analysis

Reading the texts that change the rules — laws, decrees, rulings — and explaining what they change for those who must apply them.

P2

Territories and local government

Supporting local governments with their powers, budgets and projects, where the law meets the ground.

P3

Structuring and financing

Setting up and structuring projects, and connecting them with public, private and donor financing.

P4

Firm and methodology

A written method shared by the whole team, and tools built to keep to it — Vigie is one of them.
